CALIFA (the Calar Alto Legacy Integral Field Area survey) is an integral-field spectroscopy survey of nearby galaxies led by Sebastián F. Sánchez. I was a founding member of its science team from 2010 to 2016. Participating institutions include CAHA, the Institute of Astronomy (Cambridge), IAC, IAA, the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, the Universidad Complutense de Madrid and the Institut für Astronomie, Heidelberg.

Student theses based on CALIFA include the PhD thesis "CALIFA survey Tidally-perturbed Galaxies: Effects on Star Formation" (A. Morales Vargas, Universidad de Guanajuato, 2021; co-supervisor) and the BSc thesis "Estudio y caracterización de regiones HII atípicas de la muestra del cartografiado CALIFA" (R. Luque Ramírez, Universidad de Granada, 2015; co-director). The papers whose titles name CALIFA are listed below.
