TARSET searches for fast optical transients — point sources that appear in a single exposure — on Tonantzintla Schmidt Camera plates of INAOE's Tonantzintla photographic archive (14,613 digitised plates) taken before 4 October 1957, the launch date of Sputnik 1.

It is an independent test of the [VASCO](https://scixplorer.org/abs/2025arXiv250715896V/abstract) results obtained with Palomar and Hamburg plates: a third archive, with a different instrument, site and emulsions, makes it possible to distinguish real signals from plate artefacts.

TARSET participates in the [Lost Light Project](https://lostlightproject.netlify.app/), an open international challenge in archival astronomy.
