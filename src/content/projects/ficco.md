FICCO (*Fast Imaging Camera for Cananea Observatory*) is an instrument project that I lead as Principal Investigator since 2018, carried out by INAOE and IA-UNAM. The instrument is available to the national astronomical community at the Observatorio Astrofísico Guillermo Haro (OAGH).

Project participants: E. Castillo (INAOE and SRON Netherlands Institute for Space Research), P. García-Flores (INAOE), G. Escobedo (OAGH) and J. Martínez (OAGH).

The opto-mechanical design is documented in the technical report "Diseño Opto-Mecánico del Instrumento FICCO: Fast Imaging Camera for Cananea Observatory" (C. Guerrero, F. F. Rosales-Ortega, G. Escobedo, J. Martínez, E. Sainz and E. Félix; Reporte técnico No. 666, Coordinación de Astrofísica, INAOE, 2019).
