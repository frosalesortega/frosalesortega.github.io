The Telescopio San Pedro Mártir (TSPM) is a 6.5 m telescope project, led by William Lee (IA-UNAM) in collaboration with INAOE, U. Arizona, the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory and Harvard University. I was Project Scientist from 2012 to 2018.

The project was funded by CONACYT (224003, 264173, 272027 and 248500), INAOE and UNAM. It reached the Critical Design Review (CDR) phase and was paused in 2018.
