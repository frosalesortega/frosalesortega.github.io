AMUSING (*All-weather MUse Supernova Integral field Nearby Galaxies survey*) is a VLT/MUSE survey led by Joe Anderson (ESO). I have been a collaborator since 2016. Participating institutions are ESO, U. Pittsburgh, U. Chile, U. La Plata, IAA-Spain, UNAM, INAOE, Carnegie Observatories, U. Warwick and Ohio State University.

A BSc thesis that I supervise (D. García Santiago, Universidad Autónoma Benito Juárez de Oaxaca) uses the AMUSING catalogue: "Identificación y análisis de regiones HII con emisión en [SIII] a partir del catálogo AMUSING".
