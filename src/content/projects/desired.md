## Overview

DESIRED (*DEep Spectra of Ionized REgions Database*) is an international collaboration led by José Eduardo Méndez-Delgado (IA-UNAM), involving IA-UNAM, INAOE and the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias (IAC). It has been running since 2024.

## Role

I am a collaborator in the project and lead author of the first calibrations paper, "The DESIRED strong-line calibrations: I. New empirical metallicity relations for the local and high-redshift universe" (*Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society*, published online / in press, 2026; [doi:10.1093/mnras/stag1963](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stag1963)), written with 18 co-authors.

## The calibrations paper

The paper presents empirical optical strong-line metallicity calibrations based on DESIRED, a compilation of H II regions and galaxies with direct electron-temperature determinations. Physical conditions and abundances are derived homogeneously, using up-to-date atomic data. The result is 27 calibrations covering line ratios of oxygen, nitrogen, sulphur, argon and neon, including four diagnostics not calibrated before. Validity ranges and intrinsic dispersions are reported. Preprint: [arXiv:2604.16273](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16273).
