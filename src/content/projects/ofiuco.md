OFIUCO (*Optical Fourier-transform Imaging Unit for Cananea Observatory*) was an instrument project carried out by INAOE and IA-UNAM from 2016 to 2018. I was Principal Investigator and Technical Manager. It was funded by a CONACyT infrastructure grant (INFR-271124) of MXN 4,876,000 (≈ USD 261,000). I am head of INAOE's Fourier Spectroscopy Laboratory (2016 to present).

Related items: the UAM–Santander inter-university cooperation project with Latin America "Estudio de la formación y evolución de galaxias con espectroscopía bidimensional de nueva generación: OFIUCO" (PI: Yago Ascasibar, UAM; I was co-PI and National Coordinator; €12,570 ≈ USD 14,000; April 2017 to May 2018; UAM, INAOE and IA-UNAM), and the MSc thesis "Modelado de la sensibilidad espectrofotométrica de OFIUCO" (Y. del R. Borroel Duran, INAOE, 2021), for which I was principal advisor.
