PINGS (the *PPAK IFS Nearby Galaxies Survey*) is an integral-field spectroscopy survey of nearby galaxies, one of the first of its kind. I was Principal Investigator (2007–2010). Participating institutions: the Institute of Astronomy (University of Cambridge), the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, the Institut für Astronomie (Heidelberg), the Centro Astronómico Hispano-Alemán de Calar Alto and INAOE.

The survey paper and the three papers on NGC 628 are listed below. The IDL package PINGSOFT, written for the visualisation and manipulation of integral-field data, is described on the software page.
