SELGIFS (*Study of Emission Line Galaxies with Integral Field Spectroscopy*) was a project led by Ángeles I. Díaz (Universidad Autónoma de Madrid) and funded by the Marie Curie International Research Staff Exchange Scheme (FP7-PEOPLE-2013-IRSES-612701; €237,000, ≈ USD 315,000). I was co-PI and National Coordinator. The project ran from 2013 to 2017 with the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid, INAOE, the Leibniz Institute for Astrophysics Potsdam (AIP) and partners in Porto.

I was also co-PI of a related UAM–Santander project with Latin America (€12,570 ≈ USD 14,000; 2017–2018).
