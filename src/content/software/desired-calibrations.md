A Python package for deriving gas-phase oxygen abundances, 12 + log(O/H), from optical strong emission-line ratios. It provides 27 calibrations (19 monotonic and 8 double-valued), two abundance scenarios (t² = 0 and t² ≥ 0) and Monte Carlo error propagation. It is released under the MIT licence and accompanies the first calibrations paper of the DESIRED collaboration, "The DESIRED strong-line calibrations: I. New empirical metallicity relations for the local and high-redshift universe" (*Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society*, published online / in press, 2026).
