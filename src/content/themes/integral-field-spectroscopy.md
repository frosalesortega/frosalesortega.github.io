---
title: "Integral-field spectroscopy of nearby galaxies"
order: 3
summary: "Spatially resolved spectroscopy of galaxy discs, from survey design to analysis tools."
---
Integral-field spectroscopy records a spectrum at every position across a galaxy, making it possible to study stellar populations, ionised gas and kinematics together. The practical questions include how to design surveys, reduce and analyse the data, and handle large cubes of spectra. I have worked on this through PINGS, CALIFA and SELGIFS, in papers such as "PINGS: the PPAK IFS Nearby Galaxies Survey" and "PPAK wide-field Integral Field Spectroscopy of NGC 628 - II. Emission line abundance analysis", and in the software PINGSOFT.
