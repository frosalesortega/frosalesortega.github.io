---
title: "Chemical abundances & metallicity calibrations"
order: 1
summary: "Measuring the heavy-element content of ionised gas and testing the calibrations used to infer it."
---
Heavy elements in galaxies are inferred from nebular emission lines. Direct methods rely on electron temperatures measured from auroral lines; empirical calibrations extend the analysis to spectra where those lines are not detected. The questions concern how reliable such calibrations are and what they imply for galaxy chemical evolution. Related work includes "The O3N2 and N2 abundance indicators revisited: improved calibrations based on CALIFA and Te-based literature data", "A New Scaling Relation for H II Regions in Spiral Galaxies", and, within DESIRED, "The DESIRED strong-line calibrations: I".
