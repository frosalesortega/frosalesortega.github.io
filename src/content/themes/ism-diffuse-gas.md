title: "Interstellar medium & diffuse ionised gas"

summary: "Physical conditions and ionisation mechanisms of the warm ionised gas in and around star-forming galaxies."
---
Ionised gas in galaxies includes bright H II regions and a more diffuse component extending through discs and above the plane. Open questions concern which sources and processes ionise this diffuse gas, and how its properties differ from those of H II regions. The approach combines spatially resolved emission-line maps with diagnostic line ratios. My related projects and papers include BETIS, "Bidimensional Exploration of the warm-Temperature Ionised gaS (BETIS). I. Showcase sample and first results", and its second paper, on the ionisation mechanism of the extraplanar gas.
