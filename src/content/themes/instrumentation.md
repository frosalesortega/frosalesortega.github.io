Observational programmes depend on instruments and facilities that must be specified, designed, tested and made available to users. My involvement spans the scientific leadership of a large telescope project, the development of instruments for a mid-sized observatory, and participation in testing a space spectrograph. Related projects are TSPM (the 6.5 m San Pedro Mártir Telescope), OFIUCO, FICCO, the cryogenic testing of JWST-NIRSpec, and the teaching of observational optical and infrared astronomy at graduate level.
